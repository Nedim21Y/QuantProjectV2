Common questions

How often do the rankings update?
Once a week. The model scores the universe after Friday's close, so a fresh ranking is in place before the following week opens.
Why is a highly ranked stock not in the book?
The strategy holds 25 positions and allows at most 8 from any one sector. Once a sector is full, the next stock from it is skipped in favour of the best available name elsewhere. This is deliberate: without the limit the book becomes a single-sector bet.
Is this investment advice?
No. QuantApp is educational and informational only. It does not place trades, connect to a brokerage, or tell you what to buy. Quantitative output is one input among many, and you are responsible for your own decisions.
Where do the historical results come from?
A walk-forward backtest over 27 out-of-sample quarters. The model is retrained on past data only and tested on the period that follows, never on data it has seen. The Telemetry screen shows the resulting track record.
Are the backtest results guaranteed?
No. Past performance does not predict future results. The backtest also carries survivorship bias, because the stock universe is built from companies that are in the index today, so it likely overstates what live trading would have returned. This limitation is stated in the app.
Do you collect my data?
No. There is no login and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
