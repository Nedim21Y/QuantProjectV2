Privacy Policy

QuantApp. Last updated 8 August 2026.

QuantApp is an educational analytics tool. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle.

Data we collect

None that identifies you. QuantApp has no accounts, no login and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or any financial account.

What the app does

The app requests read-only data from our backend server: stock rankings, a historical backtest, and educational content. These requests contain no personal information. We do not build a profile of you.

Data stored on your device

The app may cache the most recent server response so it can display quickly. This stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Analytics and tracking

QuantApp contains no third-party advertising, no tracking SDKs and no cross-app tracking. We do not sell or share data, because we do not collect personal data.

Server logs

Our server may keep standard, short-lived technical logs such as request timestamps and error information, in order to keep the service running and secure. These are not used to identify individuals and are not linked to any profile.

Children

QuantApp is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Not financial advice

QuantApp is for education and information only. It is not investment advice and not a recommendation to buy or sell any security. You are responsible for your own decisions.
